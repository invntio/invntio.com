# Surface: invntio.com (home + legal pages, EN `/` and ES `/es/`)

Mode: Persuade (home). Read (legal pages inherit the same world).
Audience: owners of small and mid-sized businesses in Latin America, founders, and US companies choosing a software vendor. Secondary: a Stripe reviewer checking disclosures.
Action: contact Invntio (form, email, WhatsApp). Proof: live products Beavo, Axen and Bohío, plus client work for GMG. Constraints: no invented claims or testimonials; contact and policies are always reachable; placeholders stay until the user confirms.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Invntio is presented as a shipping record, not a pitch: what gets built, what is live today, and on what terms. It refuses the centered gradient hero with blurred blobs and icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white neutral ground (#f6f7f5), ink #15181c, one brand green (#2cac7b), used as the "live" signal and the primary action and nowhere decorative. Hairline rules (#e2e4e0) replace cards and shadows. Schibsted Grotesk sets everything, flush left on a 12-column grid with tabular figures. Status marks ● live, ✓ included, ○ not included / on request form the only state vocabulary, and they mean the same thing on every surface.

STORY: The visitor learns what Invntio builds (5 services), sees proof that it ships (live products with domains), understands the 4-step engagement and its payment terms, finds contact details, and trusts the plain-language policies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the wordmark on the left and Services, Work, Process, Contact and EN/ES on the right. The left 7 columns hold a small label ("Software studio · Pennsylvania, US"), a headline about 72px at desktop ("We design, build and run software for your business."), one line of specifics, a green primary button "Start a project" and a secondary link "hello@…". The right 5 columns hold a ruled "Live now" register listing Beavo, Axen, Bohío and GMG, each row showing a green ●, the name, what it is and the domain.

FORM: Release Record, changelog and deploy status board. Grounded list position 7; seed key feb21b77.
Raises: state is a mark, not a hue (cutting bench); committed scale contrast (shader portal); the process is a procession of four stages on one rail (automata); plan maps tell the truth, included vs not included (ekiben).
Signature interaction: the "Live now" register rows reveal their domain link on hover and focus, and the process rail fills stage by stage as it scrolls into view (reduced motion shows it filled).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
