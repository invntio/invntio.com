# Appsung Mobile Phones (Harrisburg, PA): online presence audit

Research date: 2026-10-06. Public, business-level information only. Nobody was contacted, no form was submitted, nothing was posted and no account was created.
Referred by Felix Junior (Global Merchants Group). His summary: a successful owner who sells phones, might want "a website or something", and rarely answers messages.

## TL;DR

- **The store is real, busy and well loved in its community.** It has a 4.8 rating from 48 Google reviews and opened about 10 months ago. Its TikTok is strong: 30.1K followers, 900K likes and posts several times a week.
- **It has no working website.** The Google listing links to `appsungmobiles.ae`, the Dubai sister company's site. That domain now shows a hosting "parking page", so every click from Google lands on a dead page. There is no US domain at all: `appsungmobiles.com`, `appsungmobile.com` and `appsungusa.com` are all unregistered.
- **Leads leak in the comments and DMs.** Under the Harrisburg launch video, people ask for prices ("15 pro max ko price kati?"), ask "dollar or dirham?", ask for the phone number, and write "I sent a DM, please reply" ("Dm gareko xu reply dinus"). Prices only live in video captions. There is no catalog, no WhatsApp link, no way to reserve a phone and no shipping checkout, even though captions say "Shipping available".
- **There is a reputation risk on Google.** The owner answered a 1-star review with a profane, insulting reply that is still public. Most other replies were posted in one batch about 2 months ago.
- **Correction to the brief.** Everything public points to a **Nepali** owner and a Nepali diaspora customer base, not an Indian one: Nepali-language posts, #nepaleseinusa, Dashain offers, and the Dubai store bills itself as the "first Nepali-owned phone shop in Dubai". Don't open with "Indian". Use "Namaste" and English.
- **Verdict:** this is a good fit. The main problem is digital sales capture, not awareness. Call first, then visit if you get a meeting. Lead with the broken website link (a free fix) and the leads lost in comments and DMs.

## 4. How customers reach them, and where inquiries get lost

Customers reach them today in four ways: walk-ins (most of the business, according to reviews), the store phone, TikTok comments and DMs, and word of mouth in the Nepali community, including buyers who carry phones home to Nepal for Dashain.

Inquiries get lost in five places:
1. **TikTok comments and DMs.** Price questions sit in public comments for days. Viewers ask "number?" and write "I sent a DM, please reply". This matches Felix's "rarely answers messages".
2. **No price list or catalog.** Every "how much?" becomes a DM or a call. Prices only live inside videos, which mix USD (Harrisburg) and AED (Dubai), and viewers literally ask "dollar or dirham?".
3. **No website and a broken Google link.** People who click "Website" on Google hit a parking page.
4. **No WhatsApp click-to-chat or reserve option**, even though the community lives on WhatsApp and Viber and captions mention shipping.
5. **Review replies come in batches**, and one hostile reply hurts trust with non-community customers, the market he needs in order to grow beyond his network.

## 6. Opportunities for Invntio (ranked by value × ease)

1. **Quick win: fix the Google profile and a one-page site (high value, very easy).** Register `appsungmobiles.com`, build a one-page site (hours, map, warranty, repair list, WhatsApp button), and point the Google "Website" field at it. Clean up the profile (address naming, services, products), set up a review-reply routine and remove or replace the hostile reply. This is the door-opener, roughly 1 week of work.
2. **Live price list and catalog with "Reserve on WhatsApp" (highest value, easy to medium).** Show models by battery-health grade with USD prices, stock status and a 100-day-warranty badge. Each item gets a "Reserve / Ask on WhatsApp" button with a pre-filled message, so the store gets **one link for the TikTok bio** and stops answering "price kati?" one by one. Inventory comes from a spreadsheet or phone admin, the same pattern as the **GMG catalog**. If he buys from We Sell Cellular or GMG, a spreadsheet import with markup works (see `gmg-wesellcellular-sync.md`). Possibly link to Felix's wholesale stock as "available to order".
3. **Instant replies on comments and DMs (high value, medium).** Auto-reply to TikTok and Instagram DMs and to WhatsApp Business with the price-list link, the address and the hours in English and Nepali, and collect leads in one inbox. Note: TikTok's DM API is limited, so the realistic setup is a WhatsApp Business catalog plus a link in the bio and a pinned comment. Check feasibility before promising.
4. **Shipping orders and deposits (medium value, medium).** Captions already say "Shipping available". Add a simple checkout or deposit (Stripe or Square) for out-of-state Nepali buyers, with IMEI and warranty registration.
5. **Multi-location brand site, US plus UAE (medium value, medium).** One brand site with US, Dubai and Ajman location pages. That would also revive the dead .ae site. Upsell later.
6. Repair booking: low priority. Reviews show walk-ins work fine.

To be honest about what already works: his **social reach is strong, and better than most competitors'**. Don't pitch "more followers". Pitch **"turn the views into sales without you answering every DM"**.

## 7. Recommendation: call or visit

- **Call first, visit second.** Felix says he ignores messages, but the store phone (717-655-6977) rings inside a store he works himself, since reviews describe him serving customers personally. A 5-minute call costs nothing. Ask for **Navin**. Once he agrees to a time, make the 1.5–2 hour drive and bring a laptop demo.
- **When to call:** Tue–Thu, **10:15–11:30 AM** (just after opening, before the lunch and after-work rush), or **3–4:30 PM**. Avoid weekends and evenings, when walk-ins peak. Note that **Dashain falls in mid to late October** and Tihar follows in early November. He is running a Dashain sale now and may be very busy or traveling, so call this week or wait until mid-November.
- **If you visit unannounced**, go Tue or Wed around 10:30 AM, and only combine it with another reason to be in Harrisburg.
