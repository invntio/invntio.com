# Nature Electric Impor-Ter, SRL — pre-call audit

Prepared 2026-10-06 for a sales call. Public, business-level info only. Nothing was contacted, submitted or posted.

## TL;DR

- **The business is effectively invisible online.** Searches for "Nature Electric" / "Impor-Ter" / "Importer", the phone (809) 867-4472, and the owner's name with San Juan de la Maguana turned up no website, no Google Business Profile, and no indexed Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or marketplace listing. Its only known channel is WhatsApp, with the status quoted below.
- From the WhatsApp status, it's a **B2B-leaning electrical-materials supplier** ("tenemos materiales eléctricos para suplir todos los proyectos", for big and small projects). The "Impor-Ter" in the name suggests it imports. It probably serves contractors and builders, with some walk-in buyers.
- **Competitors don't sell online with checkout either.** National distributors show a catalog (web or PDF) and ask you to request a quote or WhatsApp them, and they deliver nationwide. Being found on Google and having a clean catalog plus a quote flow already puts him on par with the Santo Domingo players and ahead of local San Juan shops.
- **Recommendation: the "Better" tier (≈US$900–1,200).** That's a site with a catalog, a quote/order request that goes to WhatsApp, booking for services, and a Google Business Profile. Payment links (Azul/CardNet) can be an add-on later. That matches "sell, but I handle purchases manually."

## 4. Payments for a small DR merchant (brief)

- **Azul** (Banco Popular): Link de Pagos, Payment Page, API. Needs an active RNC; approval in ~5–10 business days; commission ~4–6% depending on volume; payout 48–72h ([Vendabo](https://vendabo.com/blog/pagos-con-azul-tienda-online-republica-dominicana), [Impulsa Popular](https://impulsapopular.com/marketing/ventas/link-de-pagos-azul-vende-en-linea-sin-tener-pagina-web/), [Azul](https://www.azul.com.do/)). The payment link can be sent by WhatsApp, with no website needed.
- **CardNet**: "Enlace de pago" and hosted Botón de Pago, in DOP or USD ([Diario Libre](https://www.diariolibre.com/actualidad/contenido-patrocinado/enlace-de-pago-la-forma-mas-facil-de-concretizar-ventas-de-forma-virtual-HI19551372), [eldinero](https://eldinero.com.do/15645/pagos-con-tarjetas-de-credito-y-debito-en-republica-dominicana/)).
- Others: UepaPay (links for small businesses/social selling), tPago ([Nexux guide](https://nexux.do/pasarelas-de-pago-republica-dominicana-2025/)).
- **Stripe: not available for DR businesses** ([stripe.com/global](https://stripe.com/global)).
- **PayPal**: works in the DR but is a poor fit for local B2B: retention periods and withdrawal friction to local cards/banks are reported ([comparalatam](https://comparalatam.com/do/card/paypal.html)), and local buyers rarely use it.
- **Bank transfer** (Popular, BHD, Banreservas) is still the default for B2B orders. A site only needs to show the account details and ask for the transfer receipt on WhatsApp.

**Takeaway:** for him, a "payment" is an Azul/CardNet link sent after a manual quote, or a transfer. A full cart with integrated checkout is extra cost with little benefit.

Minimum viable. |
| **Better** ★ | Good + **cart-style quote list** ("agrega productos → envía pedido"), which arrives on WhatsApp/email with the product list and quantities; he answers with price and payment by transfer or payment link. **Contractor price list** (PDF or private page behind a code). **Appointment booking** for services/technical visits (calendar + WhatsApp reminder). Basic stats on what people ask for. | **US$1,000–1,300** (60/40) | **Best match for what he asked for**: shows services, takes appointments, "sells" while purchases stay manual. |
| **Best** | Better + Azul/CardNet payment page or links tied to the order, order statuses, WhatsApp AI assistant that answers stock/price/schedule questions from the catalog and hands off to a human, automations (quote follow-up, abandoned-request reminders, monthly contractor promo) | **US$2,000–2,800** + monthly (AI/WhatsApp API, hosting) | Nice to have. Only worth it if inquiry volume is high or he wants to stop answering repetitive questions. Needs active RNC + Azul/CardNet affiliation. |

Notes:
- Prices go in the **Better** tier only if he wants them. Most competitors hide prices. A "precio por cotización" default avoids the problem of fluctuating prices.
- An **upsell path** fits him well: start with Better, then add payment links in month 2–3 once Azul is approved, and the AI assistant once there's inquiry data.
- Monthly retainer option (hosting + catalog updates + GBP posts): ~US$30–60/mo.

## 6. Questions for the call (Spanish)

1. ¿Dónde está su tienda o almacén, y a qué zonas les vende? ¿Hacen envíos?
2. ¿Quiénes le compran más: electricistas y contratistas, constructoras, gente que está haciendo su casa, o el gobierno?
3. ¿Cuántos productos maneja más o menos, y quiere que los precios se vean en la página o prefiere que la gente le pida cotización?
4. Cuando dice "citas", ¿de qué servicios habla? ¿Visitas para medir, instalaciones, asesoría?
5. Hoy, ¿cuántos mensajes le llegan al día pidiendo precios, y quién los contesta?
6. ¿Cómo le pagan sus clientes ahora: transferencia, efectivo, tarjeta? ¿Ya tiene cuenta con Azul o CardNet?
7. ¿Tiene ya Facebook, Instagram o Google Maps del negocio, y quién va a subir los productos y las fotos: usted o nosotros?
