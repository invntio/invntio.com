# Outreach batch 2: teams without a visible instant-response stack

Prepared 2026-10-05. Research and drafts only: nothing has been sent, submitted or called.

## Why this batch looks different

Batch 1 showed that teams who buy leads **and** run Follow Up Boss with agent routing already answer instantly. Hammond Homes pays $500–700 a lead and routes it through FUB to about 10 agents; Jersey Property Group sent an automatic FUB email about 10 minutes after the form. Those teams don't have the pain. This batch aims at where the pain may still be:

- **(a)** teams that buy leads, run ads or promise leads to agents, but have **no CRM-integrated site**
- **(b)** teams with a big Instagram/Facebook audience, where **DMs never reach a CRM**

**Selection criteria:** not contacted in batch 1 (`sent_on` empty and none of drafts 1–15); PA/NJ/NY; 3–15 agents or a high producer (a few large teams at 16–17 are kept and flagged); some sign of buying or generating leads (join pages promising leads, ad pixels, text funnels, valuation funnels, Zillow profiles); and **no instant-response stack visible in the homepage code**.

**How the stack was checked:** each homepage's HTML was fetched with a browser user agent (curl; the rendered DOM for Lofty-protected sites) and searched for: FUB pixel (`widgetbe.com`, `followupboss`), kvCORE/BoldTrail/Inside Real Estate, Lofty/Chime, Brivity/PLACE, Real Geeks, Sierra Interactive, CINC, BoomTown, Ylopo, LeadConnector/GoHighLevel, Luxury Presence, chat widgets (Intercom, tawk.to, LiveChat, Tidio, Podium, Drift) and WhatsApp links. The Meta Pixel (`fbevents.js`) was recorded as a sign of ad spend. Meta Ad Library searches are exact-phrase searches on the team name, so "none found" is not proof of no ads.

**Limits to keep in mind:**

- A clean homepage doesn't prove there's no CRM. Luxury Presence, Wix and Squarespace forms can be forwarded server-side, and KW teams have Command.
- Hard lead-buying evidence and an instant-response stack tend to go together: every team with confirmed Zillow Flex or Ylopo ads showed FUB, Ylopo or Lofty. So this batch accepts weaker lead-buying evidence in exchange for no visible stack. Ask the qualifying question ("how much do you spend a month on leads, and how many do you get?") early.
- Instagram handles were checked logged out (they exist, and follower/post counts were recorded), but **2026 posting dates could not be verified**. Glance at each profile before sending.

**Message rules used:** question first, no meeting ask, under 300 characters, an observation that is true and not flattering, no mention of the studio. WhatsApp is not mentioned. Spanish versions are only for the two Spanish-market teams, to use only if the account posts in Spanish.
