# Interview script (Mom Test) for real estate agents and teams

Format: 15-minute phone or video call, held remotely (Víctor works from home). Updated 2026-10-05 after the first batch (see "Lessons from batch 1" at the end).
**Goal:** learn how this team handles leads today and what doing it badly costs them. **No selling.** Don't mention the product or prices, and don't say "would you buy…?".

---

## 1. Opening (cold)

Open with **one question they can answer right away**. Don't ask for a meeting and don't lead with "I run a software studio". Ask for the 15 minutes only at the end, if they're talking.

**Instagram DM (EN)**
> Hi [name], [one specific, true observation from their profile or site]. Quick question from someone researching how real estate teams work: when a buyer DMs your team on Instagram on a Saturday night, who actually answers it? — Víctor, Invntio

**Instagram DM (ES)**, only if the account posts in Spanish
> Hola [nombre], [observación concreta y real]. Una pregunta rápida de alguien que está investigando cómo trabajan los equipos inmobiliarios: cuando un comprador les escribe por Instagram un sábado en la noche, ¿quién le responde? — Víctor, Invntio

**Phone**
> Hi, is this [name]? … Hi [name], Víctor here. I sent you a message on [Instagram / your website] earlier. Quick question, 30 seconds and I'll let you go: when someone messages your team on a Saturday night on Instagram or Facebook, who actually answers it?

- **Call screening (Siri asks who's calling):** "Víctor, following up on my message, quick question about how your team handles weekend inquiries." Your name alone gets you declined.
- **Voicemail:** "Hi [name], Víctor here. I sent you a note today with a quick question about how your team handles messages after hours. Easiest is to reply to my note. Thanks!"
- **If someone else answers the team line:** ask for the leader; if they're out, ask the question to whoever answered.
- **Close, only if they're talking:** "This is really helpful. Could I grab 15 more minutes Wednesday or Thursday to hear how your team does it?"

If they ask why: "I build software for small businesses, and I'm talking to teams before deciding what to build. Nothing to sell yet." / "Hago software para pequeños negocios y estoy hablando con equipos antes de decidir qué construir. Nada que vender todavía."

## 2. Questions (~10)

Ask about past, concrete events. Ask for numbers, even rough ones. Follow up with "Can you tell me more about that?" / "¿Me cuenta más?"

| # | EN | ES | What you're listening for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk me through the last new lead you got. Where did it come from, and what happened from the moment it arrived? | Cuénteme del último lead nuevo que les llegó. ¿De dónde vino y qué pasó desde que entró? | Lead sources and the real flow |
| 2 | Where do most of your leads come from today? Roughly how many a month? | ¿De dónde vienen hoy la mayoría de sus leads? ¿Más o menos cuántos al mes? | Volume, and the mix of paid vs. organic |
| 3 | Who answers a new lead, and how long does it usually take? When did you last check? | ¿Quién responde un lead nuevo y cuánto tarda normalmente? ¿Cuándo fue la última vez que lo midieron? | Real response time (not the aspirational one) |
| 4 | What happened with the last lead that came in at 10 p.m. or on a Sunday? | ¿Qué pasó con el último lead que entró a las 10 de la noche o un domingo? | Gap after hours |
| 5 | How much do you spend per month on leads (Zillow, Realtor.com, ads, referral fees)? | ¿Cuánto gastan al mes en leads (Zillow, Realtor.com, anuncios, fees de referido)? | **Budget that already exists** |
| 6 | What tools or people do you already pay for to handle leads — CRM, ISA, VA, an AI tool? What do you like and what drives you crazy? | ¿Qué herramientas o personas ya pagan para manejar leads (CRM, ISA, asistente, alguna IA)? ¿Qué les gusta y qué los vuelve locos? | Current spend and frustration with alternatives |
| 7 | Tell me about a deal you lost because someone else got to the lead first. What was it worth? | Cuénteme de algún negocio que perdieron porque otro agente llegó primero al lead. ¿Cuánto valía? | Cost of a lost lead, in dollars |
| 8 | How many of your leads are Spanish-speaking or prefer another language? What happens with them today? | ¿Cuántos de sus leads hablan español o prefieren otro idioma? ¿Qué pasa con ellos hoy? | Whether being bilingual matters (don't assume it does) |
| 9 | Have you tried to fix lead response before? What did you try, and why did you keep it or drop it? | ¿Han intentado resolver la respuesta a leads antes? ¿Qué probaron y por qué lo mantuvieron o lo dejaron? | Whether they actively looked for a fix (real pain) or not |
| 10 | If this were solved, how would you know? What number would change? | Si esto estuviera resuelto, ¿cómo lo sabrían? ¿Qué número cambiaría? | The outcome metric for an outcome-based offer |
| 11 | When someone DMs you on Instagram or Facebook, what happens? Does it end up in your CRM? | Cuando alguien les escribe por Instagram o Facebook, ¿qué pasa? ¿Termina en su CRM? | Leads that never reach the CRM (Alejandra's pain) |
| 12 | What still bugs you about how you handle leads today? | ¿Qué todavía les molesta de cómo manejan los leads hoy? | Where the next opportunity is, especially when they "already solved it" |
| 13 | Who else on your team or in your market should I talk to? Would you introduce me? | ¿Con quién más de su equipo o de su mercado debería hablar? ¿Me presentaría? | Referrals = real commitment |

**Avoid:** "Would you use…?", "Would you pay $X?", "Isn't it a problem that…?" (these lead the answer and get polite "yes" replies).

## 3. What to record after each call

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Team / agents / market | 8 agents, Bucks County |
| Leads per month and sources | ~120: Zillow Flex 60%, Meta 25%, web 15% |
| Monthly spend on leads | $4,000 (self-reported) |
| Measured response time | "around 30 min during the day, next morning at night" |
| What they already pay for | Follow Up Boss + part-time ISA |
| Lost deal mentioned | Yes: ~$9K commission, Sept |
| Spanish-speaking share | ~15% |
| Commitment signal | Asked for a second call / introduced someone / shared real data / none |

**Signs it's a real buyer:** they already spend $1,000+/month on leads, there's an after-hours gap they admit to, they pay for an ISA or CRM that frustrates them, and they **ask for a follow-up on their own**. Compliments ("great idea") are not a signal.

**Reminders:** no product mentions during the interview; take notes only with permission; don't ask for their clients' data; don't cite Invntio metrics or testimonials (there aren't any).

## Lessons from batch 1 (2026-10-05)

- **Teams that buy leads may already have it solved.** Hammond pays $500–700 per lead and routes every one through Follow Up Boss to 10 agents who answer immediately; Jersey Property Group answers forms automatically through FUB. When they say "we don't have that problem", don't explain the product: ask questions 2, 11 and 12.
- **WhatsApp is "usually a scam"** for mainstream US teams. Mention it only with teams that offer it publicly (often Spanish-speaking or immigrant markets).
- **Question first, meeting later.** Asking for 15 minutes up front sounded like a sales call ("I'll call you back"). Opening with the question got people talking.
- **"Text me" means SMS**, not WhatsApp.
