# Project Diagnostic: follow-up emails

Templates for the two emails that follow a paid Project Diagnostic (Cal.com event `cal.com/invntio/diagnostic`, USD 95, 30 min). Agents drafting these emails: read this whole file first.

The diagnostic answers one question for an owner who is not technical: "what does my project need?". The deliverable is a verdict, not a plan: the right approach, the tools it needs and a rough budget. Scope, risks, timeline and a fixed price belong in the proposal.

(The Technical Session, `cal.com/invntio/technical-session`, USD 297, 60 min, is hands-on help with something the client already has. It has no written deliverable and no follow-up template; it is not credited toward a project.)

## The sequence

1. **Session** (30 min, Cal Video). The booking form gives you: name, email, company, website, what they want to build or improve, budget range, preferred language, notes.
2. **Recommendation email**, within 2 business days. This is the paid deliverable: a one-page recommendation in the body of the email. Always sent, whether or not the client continues.
3. **Proposal email**, only if the client says yes. It carries the formal written proposal: fixed scope, fixed price, milestones and payment schedule.

Keep 2 and 3 separate by default. The recommendation gives a **rough budget range**; the proposal gives a **fixed price**, scope and timeline that the client accepts. They can be combined only when the project is small and fully clear after the session; then attach the proposal to the recommendation email and say so in the "Next step" line.

## Rules for both emails

- Language: the client's "Preferred language" answer (English by default, Spanish if chosen). Plain language for a business owner, no jargon. Name tools or technology only where the recommendation needs them, and say in plain words what each one does.
- Use only what was said in the session or the booking form. Never invent numbers, metrics, deadlines or features. Mark anything you need from Víctor as `[TO CONFIRM: …]`.
- Price ranges and terms must match the published terms at invntio.com/terms: proposals are valid for 30 days; milestone payments fall due on acceptance; the final payment is due before launch; 30-day warranty after launch.
- The diagnostic fee (USD 95) is credited in full if the client starts within 60 days of the session.
- Sign as "Invntio", from hello@invntio.com. No personal name unless Víctor asks.
- Short. A busy reader should get the point from the subject line and the first two lines.

## 1. Recommendation email (deliverable)

One page at most. If it doesn't fit on one screen, cut it.

```
Subject: Your project recommendation — {Company}

Hi {Name},

Thanks again for the session. Here's our recommendation.

What you want to achieve
— {goal in the client's own terms}

Recommended approach
— {custom software / an existing tool / automation, and why, in one or two lines}

Tools and technology
— {what it needs, in plain words, e.g. "an online store on Shopify" or "a small web app connected to your accounting system"}

Rough budget range: {range, e.g. USD 4,000–6,000}

Next step: if you'd like to move forward, reply to this email and we'll send a written proposal with a fixed scope, price and timeline within 3 business days. The $95 is credited toward the project if you start within 60 days.

Invntio
hello@invntio.com · invntio.com
```

## 2. Proposal email

```
Subject: Proposal: {project name} — {Company}

Hi {Name},

Attached is our proposal for {project name}, based on our recommendation from {date}.

In short
— Scope: {one line}
— Price: USD {fixed price} (the $95 diagnostic fee is already deducted)
— Timeline: {weeks}, starting {date}
— Payments: {e.g. 40% on acceptance, 30% at milestone X, 30% before launch}

The proposal is valid for 30 days. To accept, reply "Approved" or sign the attached document and we'll send the first invoice through Stripe.

Happy to walk through it on a short call if useful.

Invntio
hello@invntio.com · invntio.com
```

The proposal document itself (scope, deliverables, out of scope, milestones, payment schedule, acceptance, warranty, link to terms) is attached as a PDF.
