# Project Diagnostic: follow-up emails

Templates for the two emails that follow a paid Project Diagnostic (Cal.com event `cal.com/invntio/diagnostic`, USD 95, 30 min). Agents drafting these emails: read this whole file first.

## The sequence

1. **Session** (30 min, Cal Video). The booking form gives you: name, email, company, website, what they want to build or improve, budget range, preferred language, notes.
2. **Summary email**, within 2 business days. This is the paid deliverable. Always sent, whether or not the client continues.
3. **Proposal email**, only if the client says yes. It carries the formal written proposal: fixed scope, fixed price, milestones and payment schedule.

Keep 2 and 3 separate by default. The summary gives a **price range**; the proposal gives a **fixed price** that the client accepts. They can be combined only when the project is small and fully clear after the session; then attach the proposal to the summary email and say so in the "Next step" line.

## Rules for both emails

- Language: the client's "Preferred language" answer (English by default, Spanish if chosen). Plain language for a business owner, no jargon, no technology names unless the client used them.
- Use only what was said in the session or the booking form. Never invent numbers, metrics, deadlines or features. Mark anything you need from Víctor as `[TO CONFIRM: …]`.
- Price ranges and terms must match the published terms at invntio.com/terms: proposals are valid for 30 days; milestone payments fall due on acceptance; the final payment is due before launch; 30-day warranty after launch.
- The diagnostic fee (USD 95) is credited in full if the client starts within 60 days of the session.
- Sign as "Invntio", from hello@invntio.com. No personal name unless Víctor asks.
- Short. A busy reader should get the point from the subject line and the first two lines.

— {custom software / existing tool / automation, and why}

## 2. Proposal email

```
Subject: Proposal: {project name} — {Company}

Hi {Name},

Attached is our proposal for {project name}, based on the diagnostic summary from {date}.

In short
— Scope: {one line}
— Price: USD {fixed price} (the $95 diagnostic fee is already deducted)
— Timeline: {weeks}, starting {date}
— Payments: {e.g. 40% on acceptance, 30% at milestone X, 30% before launch}

The proposal is valid for 30 days. To accept, reply "Approved" or sign the attached document and we'll send the first invoice through Stripe.

Happy to walk through it on a short call if useful.

Invntio
hello@invntio.com · invntio.com
```

The proposal document itself (scope, deliverables, out of scope, milestones, payment schedule, acceptance, warranty, link to terms) is attached as a PDF.
