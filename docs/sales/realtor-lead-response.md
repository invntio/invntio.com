# Lead response for real estate agents: offer and validation plan

Status: **validating** (decided 2026-10-04 in the niche session). Nothing is built or published for this yet. Agents working on it: read this file first, then `docs/research/proof-and-trust.md`.

## Constraints that shaped the decision

- Víctor sells and delivers **100% from home**: no events, no travel. A baby is on the way, so family comes first.
- Income goal for Invntio alone: $4,000/month minimum, $6,000 ideal, $8,500 target, $10,000 dream. Hours: 20–25 per week ideally, 40 maximum.

- Kinds of work to avoid: ERPs like Odoo or Frappe, churches (no budget), legal (no domain knowledge). Clients to avoid: bad payers, hagglers, nitpickers, rude clients. Industries to avoid: those against his values. Use niche choice and polite "not a fit" answers; don't publish exclusion lists.

## The product

A lead-response system. Every inquiry that arrives through Zillow, the website, Instagram, WhatsApp or ads is answered **in under 1 minute, in English or Spanish, 24/7**. The system qualifies the lead, books the showing on the agent's calendar, logs everything in a CRM and follows up automatically. Built with Invntio's strengths: APIs, integrations, AI agents and automation.

The same system can later be sold to residential contractors (the research session's segment #2) with few changes. That is the fallback if agents don't validate.

## Who buys

- **Buyers:** realtor **teams of 3–15 agents**, and **high-producing agents (about 20+ sales a year) who already pay for leads**, for example Zillow Premier Agent, Realtor.com, or Facebook/Google ads. Any market; start with PA, NJ and NY.
- **Not buyers:** average solo agents. Their income is low and irregular and they're used to $50–300/month tools. Origin is not the filter. Bilingual is a feature ("also answers in Spanish"), strongest where many buyers speak Spanish.
- **Qualifying question:** "How much do you spend a month on leads, and how many do you get?" If they don't pay for leads, they aren't the client.

## Offer (draft, to test in conversations)

> Every lead answered in under 1 minute, in English or Spanish, 24/7. Showings booked on your calendar.

| Buyer | Setup | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Team (3–15 agents) | $2,500–4,000 | $497–997 |
| High-producing individual agent | $750, or $0 with a 3-month minimum | $297–397 |
| First 3 clients (pilot) | 40–50% off | same, in exchange for real data and a testimonial |

- **Guarantee:** if within 30 days it isn't answering every lead in under a minute, the next month is free. It's a **credit, not a refund**, which matches the refund policy.
- **Numbers:** for example 4 teams × $697 + 4 individuals × $347 ≈ $4,200/month recurring, plus setups.

## Validation plan (2 weeks, from home)

1. **Lead list:** realtor teams and high-producing agents in PA/NJ/NY with signs they buy leads. Requested from the research session (`Research Hispanic SMB owners in PA (LinkedIn+Maps)`), output in its repo under `docs/research/realtor-lead-response/`.
2. **Referrals by message:** ask the warm network (family, church, current clients) "Do you know a realtor team or a busy agent?". Mortgage loan officers are good allies because each one works with dozens of agents.
3. **10 video conversations** using the interview script, to listen, not to sell. Ask about how leads arrive, reply speed, after hours, **monthly spend on leads**, current tools (CRM, ISA) and the cost of a lost lead.
4. **Demo:** a 30-second video of a lead answered in Spanish on WhatsApp (motion graphics session), for prospects and not for colleagues.

### Decision criteria after the 10 conversations

- **Go:** at least 4 of 10 pay for leads (>$300/month) and admit they answer slowly or after hours, **and** at least 2 say yes to a paid pilot.
- **Pivot:** they pay for leads but already have an ISA or a tool that works. Test residential contractors with the same system.
- **Stop:** fewer than 3 of 10 pay for leads. The pain isn't strong enough for this buyer.

## Interview log

### 1. Former agent, Florida (Alejandra), 2026-10-04

- **Brokerage tools:** a website showing the company's listings as the agent's own, a CRM with email automation, flyer/postcard/banner templates, and a map app where clients can search listings. Paid as part of the annual brokerage package.
- **Clarification:** inquiries made on the brokerage-provided website do land in the CRM automatically. Leads from Instagram, WhatsApp or anywhere outside the brokerage site do **not**; the agent has to add them by hand. Building the initial contact list is also manual. A new agent's site gets little traffic.
- **Use:** the website gets used. The CRM decays because adding and updating contacts is slow and tedious, so most agents don't use it as intended. Templates go unused (Canva is better).
- **Nights and weekends:** you answered "when you could", the next day or later. No automation of any kind.
- **Where real leads came from:** mostly people who knew her, or who replied to her social media posts (personal Instagram/Facebook), **not** paid platforms. Lead generation was bundled in the brokerage fee, not bought separately.
- **ISAs:** none she knew of; some part-time office secretaries. Every agent handled leads their own way.
- **Referrals for interviews:** "maybe".

**What it changes:**
- Confirms the slow reply and the lack of automation.
- Adds a second pain: **the CRM doesn't fill itself.** Offer idea: capture every lead **from outside the brokerage site** (Instagram/Facebook DMs, WhatsApp, personal channels), reply instantly, and **log it automatically in the brokerage CRM** the agent already has. The brokerage site already covers its own inquiries.
- Confirms that average agents whose leads are bundled are not the buyer; keep the focus on teams and agents who buy leads separately.
- Social DMs (Instagram/Facebook) may matter as much as Zillow for many agents. Ask about it in every interview.
